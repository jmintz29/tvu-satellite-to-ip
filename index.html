THE CHALLENGE

Satellite to IP Transition — Why Now?

Market forces are accelerating the move to IP. The economics, logistics, and technology all point to the same conclusion: the time to transition is now.

📡

C-Band 5G Displacement

The FCC has already repurposed 280 MHz for 5G. The upper C-band inquiry is underway — remaining spectrum is at risk of further reallocation.

💲

Rising Transponder Costs

The SES-Intelsat merger ($3.1B) consolidates 96% of U.S. C-band spectrum. Fewer providers means fewer options and higher prices for broadcasters.

⏱️

Weeks to Provision

Adding a new satellite destination means shipping hardware, installing dishes, and waiting 2–4 weeks per site. IP provisioning takes hours.

⚙️

Aging Infrastructure

IRD fleets ($1,500–$2,500/unit) need replacement. RF engineers are retiring. Manual failover takes 5–15 minutes vs. sub-second with IP.
